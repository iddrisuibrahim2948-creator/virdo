Our Standards & Approach

How VIRD Foundation works, who we serve, how we protect people, how we stay accountable and how we measure impact.

1. OUR APPROACH

How We Work

VIRD follows an integrated development approach:

  1. 1.

    Listen

    Understand community priorities and lived realities.

  2. 2.

    Assess

    Use evidence and community-level data to identify needs and vulnerabilities.

  3. 3.

    Prioritize

    Identify the most important and feasible interventions.

  4. 4.

    Protect

    Ensure safeguarding, inclusion, dignity and Do No Harm.

  5. 5.

    Invest

    Mobilize resources for practical solutions.

  6. 6.

    Implement

    Work with communities and partners to deliver quality programmes.

  7. 7.

    Measure

    Track results, accountability and impact.

  8. 8.

    Learn

    Use evidence and community feedback to improve.

  9. 9.

    Sustain

    Strengthen local ownership, systems and maintenance.

  10. 10.

    Scale

    Expand successful interventions responsibly.

2. OUR COMMITMENT TO INCLUSION

Everyone Deserves Dignity and Opportunity

VIRD is committed to inclusion, equity and non-discrimination.

Our programmes seek to consider the needs of:

  • Children
  • Women and girls
  • Youth
  • Older persons
  • Persons with disabilities
  • Poor households
  • Food-insecure households
  • Unemployed and underemployed people
  • Smallholder farmers
  • Disaster-affected populations
  • Socially excluded and underserved groups

Programme participation is based on transparent and appropriate criteria such as need, vulnerability and programme relevance, rather than unfair discrimination.

3. SAFEGUARDING

Protecting People Is Our Responsibility

VIRD maintains a strong commitment to safeguarding.

We have zero tolerance for:

  • Child abuse
  • Sexual exploitation
  • Sexual abuse
  • Harassment
  • Exploitation
  • Abuse of power
  • Other forms of misconduct

We seek to ensure that beneficiaries, staff, volunteers, partners and communities can engage with VIRD safely and with dignity.

Safeguarding Principles

Safety • Dignity • Confidentiality • Accountability • Survivor-Centred Response • Do No Harm

Report a Safeguarding Concern

4. ACCOUNTABILITY

We Are Accountable to the People We Serve

VIRD believes accountability is essential to responsible humanitarian and development practice.

We seek feedback through appropriate mechanisms including:

Community meetings

Feedback channels

Complaint mechanisms

Designated focal persons

Telephone and digital channels

Programme monitoring

We use community feedback to improve programme quality and organizational performance.

5. MONITORING, EVALUATION, ACCOUNTABILITY & LEARNING

Evidence. Accountability. Learning. Impact.

VIRD's MEAL approach follows:

PLAN→IMPLEMENT→MONITOR→ANALYSE→LEARN→ADAPT→REPORT→IMPROVE

We seek to measure:

  • People reached
  • Service improvements
  • Programme quality
  • Beneficiary satisfaction
  • Inclusion and equity
  • Safeguarding
  • Resilience
  • Livelihood outcomes
  • Community participation
  • Sustainability
  • Organizational performance

Our Learning Philosophy

Evidence → Learning → Adaptation → Improved Programming → Greater Impact

11. OUR THEORY OF CHANGE

From Vulnerability to Sustainable Well-Being

We seek to ensure that humanitarian assistance becomes a pathway toward stronger households, stronger communities and greater opportunity.

VIRD FOUNDATION

THEORY OF CHANGE

FROM RELIEF TO RESILIENCE | FROM VULNERABILITY TO OPPORTUNITY

THE CHANGE WE SEEK

If vulnerable individuals and communities receive timely and dignified assistance, gain improved access to essential services, strengthen their productive and social capacities, participate meaningfully in decisions affecting them, and gain access to sustainable opportunities and resilient community systems, then they will be better able to withstand shocks, improve their well-being and move from vulnerability toward sustainable resilience and empowerment.

13. GOVERNANCE

Accountable Leadership

VIRD's governance framework provides clear separation between strategic oversight and day-to-day management.

Tier 1

Board of Directors

Provides:

  • Strategic oversight
  • Governance
  • Financial oversight
  • Risk oversight
  • Safeguarding oversight
  • Policy approval
  • Executive oversight
Tier 2

Executive Leadership

Responsible for:

  • Strategy implementation
  • Programme delivery
  • Financial management
  • Staff management

  • Resource mobilization
